About Me

My name is Lawrence, and I'm a student in the School of Engineering and Technology at Mulungushi University. Outside of lecture halls, I serve as the musical director for my church's praise team, where I teach worship songs, plan rehearsals, and play keyboard during services. I enjoy combining a technical mindset with creativity, whether that means recording a click track for my church band or, more recently, writing my very first lines of HTML. Web technologies are new territory for me, but I'm looking forward to building something that reflects who I am and sharing it with the people I study alongside.
